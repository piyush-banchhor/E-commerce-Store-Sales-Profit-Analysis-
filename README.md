# E-commerce-Store-Sales-Profit-Analysis-
# 🛒 E-Commerce Sales Analysis (Superstore Dataset)

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing an E-commerce retail dataset to uncover key business insights related to **sales, profit, customer segments, and product performance**.

The analysis is performed using Python in a Jupyter Notebook, with a strong emphasis on **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** and business-driven insights.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze overall sales and profit performance
* Identify top-performing and loss-making categories
* Understand customer segment behavior
* Explore monthly sales and profit trends
* Evaluate business efficiency using profit ratios

---

## 📂 Dataset

* Dataset Name: **Superstore Dataset**
* Contains:

  * Order details
  * Sales and profit data
  * Product categories and sub-categories
  * Customer segments
  * Shipping and regional information

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* Python 🐍
* Pandas
* Plotly
* Jupyter Notebook

---

## 🔍 Key Analysis Performed

### 1. Data Cleaning & Preprocessing

* Converted date columns into datetime format
* Extracted features like:

  * Year
  * Month
  * Day of week

---

### 2. Sales & Profit Analysis

* Total Sales: ~2.29M
* Total Profit: ~286K

📌 Insight:

* Profit margin is relatively low compared to sales

---

### 3. Category-Level Analysis

* **Technology** → Highest sales & profit
* **Office Supplies** → Stable and profitable
* **Furniture** → High sales but low profit

📌 Insight:

* Furniture category needs optimization

---

### 4. Sub-Category Analysis

* Top performers: Phones, Chairs, Storage
* Loss-making: **Tables**

📌 Insight:

* Some products generate revenue but reduce overall profit

---

### 5. Time-Based Analysis

* Monthly trends show fluctuations in sales and profit
* Higher sales observed toward year-end

📌 Insight:

* Increased sales do not always result in higher profit

---

### 6. Customer Segment Analysis

* Consumer → Highest contribution
* Corporate → Moderate performance
* Home Office → Lowest contribution

📌 Insight:

* Consumer segment is the primary revenue driver

---

### 7. Sales-to-Profit Ratio

* Evaluates efficiency of revenue generation

📌 Insight:

* Some categories have high sales but poor profitability

---

## 🚨 Key Business Insights

* Loss-making products (especially Tables) impact profitability
* Furniture category underperforms despite high sales
* Discounts may be reducing profit margins
* Revenue growth does not guarantee profit growth

---

## 💡 Recommendations

* Reduce discounts on loss-making products
* Re-evaluate pricing strategy for Furniture
* Focus on high-margin categories (Technology, Office Supplies)
* Target Consumer and Corporate segments for growth
* Optimize cost and supply chain for low-profit items
